RankLyft usage cost is driven primarily by vendor API calls (DataForSEO and Google) and by how much data you track per website.

## Pricing model

- DataForSEO usage is billed from your DataForSEO balance.
- Some DataForSEO products (Backlinks and AI Optimization) require a separate $100/month minimum commitment.

See:

- [Pricing overview](/data/pricing-overview)
- [DataForSEO cost breakdown](/data/dataforseo-costs)

## Common limits

Limits vary by plan. In practice, the most important constraints are:

- Number of websites in your account
- Number of tracked keywords per website (Position Tracking)
- Number of Google-discovered keywords shown in Organic Rankings (when GSC is connected)

<Info>
  If you hit a limit in the UI, RankLyft typically keeps collecting data but may hide or truncate what’s displayed until you upgrade.
</Info>

## Cost control checklist

- Track fewer keywords (or track only the keywords tied to client deliverables)
- Prefer depth 10 tracking unless you need to monitor positions below page one
- Avoid running Site Audits more frequently than you need
- Use on-demand refreshes when you have a specific reason (after a deployment, after a fix)
