---
title: "Managing websites"
description: "How to switch between websites, add new ones, and remove tracked domains."
---

RankLyft is built around a multi-website model. Each website you add gets its own dashboard, keyword set, and data history. You can switch between them at any time from the sidebar.

## Switching between websites

The sidebar displays all of your active websites. Click any website name to switch to it. The entire interface — dashboard, position tracking, site audit, and all other sections — updates to reflect the selected domain.

## Adding more websites

To add another website, click the **+** button at the top of the sidebar, or navigate to **/new**. Each new website goes through the same automatic data pull as your first — see [Adding a website](/getting-started/add-a-website) for a full breakdown of what gets fetched.

Every website is independent. Adding a new domain does not affect the data or settings of any other website in your account.

## Removing a website

To remove a website, open its settings and select **Delete Website**.

When you remove a website, RankLyft hides it from your sidebar and stops tracking it, but all of its data is preserved — ranked keywords, position history, top pages, domain overview snapshots, and audit results are all retained.

<Info>
  Removing a website does not permanently delete its data. Nothing is lost when you remove a site.
</Info>

## Restoring a removed website

If you need to restore a domain you previously removed, contact your PageLyft admin. Because all data is preserved on removal, restoration brings back the full history exactly as it was before the domain was removed.
