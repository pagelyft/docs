RankLyft is an SEO platform built for digital agencies. It gives you keyword tracking, site auditing, backlink analysis, and competitive research — all in one place — without the enterprise price tag.

## What RankLyft does

When you add a client website, RankLyft automatically pulls:

- **Ranked keywords** — the top 100 keywords the domain currently ranks for in Google
- **Top pages** — the 50 pages driving the most organic traffic
- **Domain overview** — authority score, estimated traffic, and backlink counts
- **Historical data** — ranking trends back to January of the prior year
- **Position tracking** — daily rank tracking for your most important keywords, starting the next morning

Beyond the initial pull, RankLyft refreshes data on a regular schedule so you always have current rankings to show clients.

## Who it's for

RankLyft is built for agencies managing SEO across multiple clients. If you're running monthly reports, tracking keyword movements, auditing technical issues, or doing competitive research across a book of clients, RankLyft is designed for that workflow.

Each website you add gets its own isolated dashboard. You can switch between clients, track independent keyword sets, and export data without mixing accounts.

## The data behind it

RankLyft uses real search data from the same underlying sources that power many enterprise SEO platforms. Because the data is billed by usage rather than by seat, RankLyft passes those savings directly to you.
