---

RankLyft does not have a self-service signup. Your account is created for you by a PageLyft admin. Once your account is ready, you can sign in at any time.

## How to sign in

1. Go to [rank.pagelyft.studio/login](https://rank.pagelyft.studio/login).
2. Enter the email address and password your admin provided.
3. Click **Sign In**.

You'll be redirected to the main dashboard after a successful sign-in.

## What to know

**Email and password only.** There is no option to sign in with Google, Apple, or any other third-party account. Use the email and password your admin set up for you.

**No self-service signup.** There is no "Create an account" option. If you need access, contact your PageLyft account manager.

**You stay signed in.** RankLyft keeps you signed in across browser sessions automatically. You won't need to re-enter your credentials every time you open the app.

**Session expiry.** If your session expires after an extended period of inactivity, RankLyft will redirect you to the sign-in page. After signing back in, you'll be returned to where you were.

## Trouble signing in

RankLyft does not have a self-service password reset. If you've forgotten your password or can't access your account, contact your PageLyft admin and they'll reset it for you.

<Warning>
  Do not share your login credentials with other team members. If multiple people at your agency need access, ask your admin to create separate accounts for each person.
</Warning>
