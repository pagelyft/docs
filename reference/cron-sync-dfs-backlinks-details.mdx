Syncs “detail” backlink data: top referring domains and backlink history time series.

## Endpoint

```text
GET /api/cron/sync-dfs-backlinks-details
POST /api/cron/sync-dfs-backlinks-details
```

**Schedule:** Monday and Thursday at 5:00 AM UTC (`0 5 * * 1,4`)
**Max duration:** 300 seconds
**Auth:** `Authorization: Bearer {CRON_SECRET}`

## What it does

For each target website, the job:

1. Writes a `data_sync_jobs` row (`source=dataforseo`, `feature=backlinks_details`)
2. Fetches top referring domains from DataForSEO and upserts into `referring_domains`
3. Fetches backlink history from DataForSEO and upserts into `backlink_history`
4. Updates `website_data_freshness` for `dataforseo/backlinks_details`

## Database tables

| Table | Operation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `data_sync_jobs` | Insert/Update | Job telemetry (status, errors) |
| `website_data_freshness` | Upsert | “Last updated” timestamps per feature |
| `referring_domains` | Upsert | Referring domains table in Backlinks |
| `backlink_history` | Upsert | Backlink history chart data |
